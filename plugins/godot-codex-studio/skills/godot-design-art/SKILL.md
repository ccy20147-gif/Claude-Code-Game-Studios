# Design Art Direction

Create an art bible linked to the baseline: visual pillars, references, palette, silhouette, material and lighting rules, accessibility/readability constraints, platform budgets, and explicit anti-references. Add import contracts for scale, axes, pivot, LOD, collision, texture budgets, naming, and reimport behavior.

Do not represent external DCC automation as generally available. For any asset requiring editor automation, consult `../../catalog/capabilities.yaml` and keep it at its stated support level. Approve the art bible before `$godot-produce-assets` creates production work.
