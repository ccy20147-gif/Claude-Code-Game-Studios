# UE5 Codex Studio

Use `.ue5-codex-studio/project.yaml` as the workflow state and treat structured YAML artifacts as authoritative. Preserve user-authored source files and record their hashes before creating adaptation artifacts.

Do not enter maintenance without an accepted production baseline or hotfix without a deployed build identity. In `solo` and `lean` review modes, save reversible design work as DRAFT and show changes plus assumptions. Ask for approval before selecting a concept, accepting a baseline, expanding scope, invoking paid or external operations, or recording a waiver. In `full` mode, confirm each design section before writing it. Within an approved work item, implement and test continuously.

Do not claim an Unreal or Blender mutation succeeded from a tool acknowledgment alone. Record the required readback evidence level, and mark timed-out operations `UNKNOWN` until reconciled.
